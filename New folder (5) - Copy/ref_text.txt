11. Project Overview
Water quality directly affects human health, agriculture, fish farming, and daily household
activities. Existing low-cost water quality devices mainly display sensor values such as
pH, TDS, and turbidity, which are difficult for ordinary users to understand. They also
cannot determine how much the water has improved after treatment or maintain a history
of water quality changes.
The proposed project,AquaView, is an AI and IoT based smart water assessment
platform that analyzes water quality, provides intelligent recommendations, compares
water quality before and after treatment, and builds a GPS-based community water
quality map.
2. Problem Statement
Many households and farmers do not have access to laboratory testing facilities. Existing
portable devices only provide raw measurements without explaining:
•Whether the water is suitable for drinking.
•Whether it can be used for irrigation.
•Whether it is suitable for fish farming.
•Why the water quality is poor.
•How to improve the water quality.
•Whether treatment has actually improved the water.
3. Objectives
The main objectives of AquaView are:
•Analyze water quality using IoT sensors.
•Determine water suitability for humans, plants, fish farming, and household use.
•Identify possible water quality issues.
•Recommend suitable treatment methods.
•Compare water quality before and after treatment.
•Calculate the percentage of improvement after treatment.
•Store historical reports in the cloud.
•Generate a GPS-based community water quality map.

Unlike traditional water quality monitoring systems, AquaView focuses on intelligent
decision support rather than only displaying sensor readings.
The proposed contributions are:

•Before and after treatment comparison.
•Water recovery verification.

•GPS-based community water quality mapping.

36. Methodology
The proposed workflow is:
1. Collect water sample.
2. Read sensor values using ESP32.
3. Send sensor data to cloud database.
4. AI analyzes water quality.
5. Generate water health report.
6. Recommend suitable treatment.
7. User applies treatment.
8. Retest the same water.
9. Compare previous and current results.
10. Update water quality map.

10. Conclusion
AquaView aims to provide an affordable AI-powered IoT solution for intelligent water
quality assessment. Instead of displaying only sensor values, the system will interpret
water quality, recommend treatments, compare retest results, estimate improvement,
and visualize community water quality through GPS mapping. The proposed system has
the potential to support households, farmers, fish cultivators, and local communities in
making better water-related decisions.
