Isolation & Voice Capture

Acoustic Vocal & Dialogue Booth

Engineered specifically for emotional vocal expression and crystalline drama dialogue ADR. The booth utilizes dual-leaf room-in-room decoupling with specialized rockwool density cores, eradicating exterior street noise from Gulshan/Niketon and eliminating room resonance flutter.

Acoustic Decoupling:

Floating neoprene base preventing floor mechanical vibrations and rumble transmission.

Absorption Calibration:

Tuned absorption coefficient from 200Hz to 16kHz for neutral, mix-ready vocal capture.

Applications:

Lead drama OST vocals, high-stakes film dubbing, poetry recitations, acoustic instruments.

Headphone Latency:

Zero-latency hardware direct monitoring for effortless performer pitch and timing.

Acoustic Purity Philosophy

Direct Microphone & Pure Signal Path

Why run a sensitive vocal signal through 50 feet of noisy console circuitry and redundant summing buses? JKH Jesan adopts modern purist signal topology: Gold-sputtered condenser diaphragm → Discrete boutique Class-A analog preamp → 24-bit 192kHz AD/DA conversion. Zero degradation, maximum emotional warmth.

Transducer Quality:

Large-diaphragm studio condenser capturing intimate vocal air, grit, and breathing dynamics.

Preamp Purity:

Discrete high-voltage rail headroom guaranteeing zero digital clipping on vocal crescendos.

Noise Floor:

Ultra-low equivalent input noise (EIN -129dBu) allowing whisper-level dialogue capture.

Digital Clocking:

Femtosecond ultra-low jitter internal clocking for smear-free transient definition.
