The Artist

Bridging Emotion & Technology

Based in Niketon, Dhaka, I am a Music Producer and Composer dedicated to the art of sound. With a deep connection to the Bangladeshi media industry, my work spans from heart-touching OSTs for Bangla Natoks to high-fidelity mixing for fictions.

Whether it's composing the background score (BGM) for a dramatic scene or mixing & mastering a track for digital platforms like Apple Music & Spotify, I bring a blend of creative intuition and technical precision to every project.
