Core Methodology

The 4 Pillars of Performance Marketing

Every campaign follows a rigorous 4-step framework to maximize Return on Ad Spend and eliminate budget waste.

Pillar 01 · Data Foundation

Server-Side Tracking Accuracy

Before spending \$1 on ads, we deploy Google Tag Manager Server Container, GA4, and Meta Conversions API (CAPI) to ensure 99.8% event attribution precision despite iOS 14.5+ limits.

Pillar 02 · Audience Architecture

Full-Funnel Audience Targeting

Segmenting cold prospecting (TOFU), warm engagement (MOFU), and high-intent cart abandoners (BOFU) prevents self-cannibalization and lowers Customer Acquisition Cost.

Pillar 03 · Creative Testing

Rapid Creative & Copy A/B Testing

Systematic testing of headlines, hooks, visual assets, and primary text variations to isolate high-performing ad concepts before scaling budget.

Pillar 04 · Budget Optimization

Daily Bid & ROAS Reallocation

Continuous monitoring of ROAS, CPA, and CPM metrics. Scaling winning ad sets while instantly killing underperforming placements.
