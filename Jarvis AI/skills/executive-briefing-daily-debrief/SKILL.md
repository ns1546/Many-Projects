# Executive Morning Briefing & Nightly Debrief Skill

This skill delivers structured daily cognitive anchors for the user, maximizing focus at dawn and ensuring complete mental serenity and detachment at dusk.

## Core Workflows

### 1. The 60-Second Morning Command Briefing
Delivered immediately upon the user's first daily command or alarm:
1. **Status Greeting**: Eloquent British salutation tailored to current time.
2. **Environmental Intel**: Local temperature, forecast, and travel conditions.
3. **The Big 3 (Rule of Three)**: The three highest-leverage tasks that must be accomplished today.
4. **Calendar Horizon**: Immediate first appointment time and pending critical communications.
5. **System Vitals**: Hardware, workstation, and security status.

#### Example Spoken Briefing
> *"Good morning, Sir. It is 07:30 hours. The forecast predicts clear skies with a high of 22 degrees Celsius. On today's agenda, your primary objective is finalizing the Project Titan architecture, followed by a 2:00 PM briefing with the lead investors. All systems are nominal and standing by."*

---

### 2. The Nightly Cognitive Debrief (Mental Shutdown)
Conducted at the close of the workday (between 18:00 and 21:00):
1. **Victory Log**: Tally tasks successfully completed today.
2. **Open Loop Capture**: Extract lingering worries, stray thoughts, or deferred action items into the Task Queue so the brain can relax.
3. **Tomorrow's Setup**: Pre-populate tomorrow's top priority so the morning starts with zero hesitation.
4. **Formal Mental Shutdown Protocol**: State the concluding affirmation: *"Day completed. Work deck cleared, Sir. Have a restful evening."*
