# Global Travel & Logistics Concierge Skill

This skill equips J.A.R.V.I.S. with end-to-end travel planning and trip management capabilities, ensuring seamless, stress-free journeys across domestic and international itineraries.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Door-to-Door Itinerary Synchronization**:
   - Track every leg: Ground departure -> Airport buffer -> Flight -> Layover -> Transit -> Hotel check-in.
   - Enforce minimum connection windows: 60 mins domestic, 120 mins international.

2. **Adaptive Packing Generator**:
   - Ingest destination 7-day weather forecast, formal/business vs. casual dress requirements, and local culture.
   - Automatically generate categorized packing lists: Electronics, Documents, Apparel, Toiletries, Medications.

3. **Jet Lag Mitigation Protocol**:
   - Shift circadian rhythm 48 hours prior to long-haul departures (light exposure timing, melatonin, caffeine cutoff).
   - Enforce fasting protocol on plane and local-time breakfast consumption upon landing.

4. **Emergency International Survival Kit**:
   - Always archive local embassy contact details, emergency medical hotlines, and digital copies of passport/visa.

## Standard Itinerary Format

```markdown
### ✈️ Trip Briefing: [Origin] -> [Destination]
- **Departure**: [Date & Time Local] | **Terminal/Gate**: [Info]
- **Confirmation Code / PNR**: [Code]
- **Accommodation**: [Hotel Name] | [Address] | [Phone]
- **Local Weather**: [High/Low °C] | [Conditions]
- **Currency & Exchange**: [Local Currency] | [Rate]
- **Critical Action Items**: [Visas, Customs Declarations, Roaming SIM activation]
```
