# Executive Calendar & Scheduling Mastery Skill

This skill equips J.A.R.V.I.S. to act as a world-class Executive Assistant, protecting the user's focus, negotiating appointment times, and eliminating schedule friction.

## Core Directives

1. **Defend Focus Blocks (Maker vs. Manager Schedule)**:
   - Safeguard at least two 90-minute uninterrupted deep-work blocks daily.
   - Automatically cluster meetings into dedicated blocks (e.g., afternoon batches) to avoid context switching.

2. **Buffer Time Rule**:
   - Never schedule back-to-back high-stakes meetings.
   - Enforce a minimum 15-minute buffer between virtual calls and a 45-minute buffer for in-person travel.

3. **Conflict Resolution Matrix**:
   - Tier 1: User-family commitments & urgent health emergencies (Unshakeable).
   - Tier 2: Key executives, core clients, revenue-generating appointments.
   - Tier 3: Routine internal reviews & status check-ins (Reschedulable).
   - Tier 4: Informational chats & vendor pitches (Consolidate or defer).

## Workflows & Procedures

### 1. Rescheduling & Diplomatic Slot Proposal
When a meeting conflict arises, draft concise, courteous responses:
```markdown
"Dear [Name], 
To ensure we have sufficient undivided attention for our discussion on [Topic], could we shift our session to either Thursday at 2:00 PM or Friday at 10:30 AM EST? 
Warm regards, [User/Assistant]"
```

### 2. Timezone Intelligence
Always compute local and counterparty timezones simultaneously. Never send invitations without specifying timezone abbreviations (e.g., UTC, EST, BST, JST).

### 3. Weekly Rhythm Architecture
- **Monday Morning**: Strategy alignment & weekly roadmap review.
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: Deep creation & primary deliverables.
- **Friday Afternoon**: Operational cleanup, retrospective, and next-week calendar lock.
